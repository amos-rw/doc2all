Five rules this design depends on

These are my proposals. Tell me if one is wrong.

  • One door for files.Every file comes in through the upload gate.
  • The runner knows little.One copy, one job id, no database.
  • One owner check.One place asks who owns what.
  • Two steps to sign in.A password is never enough. A passkey is preferred.
  • Outside is off.Only email is used, and only for codes and notices. Anything else needs a yes.
